## Key Features

* **Real participants** — all data captured from real individuals, not synthetic or scraped
* **Multi-image per identity** — multiple photos per person for within-identity learning
* **Multi-ethnic coverage** — Caucasian, Black, East Asian, South Asian, Middle Eastern, Latin American
* **Multi-device capture** — iOS and Android phones, multiple device models
* **Diverse environments** — real-world lighting, backgrounds, and capture conditions
* **Commercial license** — full datasets licensed for AI model training, validation, and deployment

## Full Commercial Collection

The full Axon Labs face recognition library covers every major face data use case. Each individual dataset can be licensed separately or in combination:

### Identity Verification & KYC

* **[Selfies and Paired ID Photos](https://axonlab.ai/dataset/selfies-and-id-photo/)** — 70,000+ images from 6,000+ participants, 10–15 selfies + 2 ID photos per person, designed for KYC and 1:1 face verification

### Face Recognition at Scale

* **[NIST Face Recognition Dataset](https://axonlab.ai/dataset/nist-dataset/)** — large-scale dataset for [NIST FRVT](https://pages.nist.gov/frvt/) 1:1 verification and 1:N identification benchmarking, multi-ethnic coverage with deduplicated identities

### Multi-Modal Face Recognition

* **[Selfies & Videos Face Recognition Dataset](https://axonlab.ai/dataset/selfie-and-video-dataset/)** — 10,000+ files from 1,000+ participants combining still photos and video footage per identity for video face recognition training

### Age Verification & Minor Detection

* **[Biometric Age Estimation Dataset for Minors](https://axonlab.ai/dataset/age-estimation-minors-face-dataset/)** — 10,000+ images covering age range 10–30 for age gating and minor verification model training
* **[Synthetic Children Faces](https://axonlab.ai/dataset/synthetic-children-faces/)** — AI-generated children faces for deepfake detection and bias-safe age model training

## Use Cases

* **KYC and identity verification** — selfie-to-document matching for banking onboarding and regulated industries
* **1:1 face verification** — access control, device unlock, payment authorization, multi-factor authentication
* **1:N face identification** — surveillance, border control, enterprise security, large-scale gallery search
* **NIST FRVT submission preparation** — meet the scale and demographic diversity required for competitive benchmark performance
* **Age estimation and minor verification** — age gating for regulated content and services
* **Demographic bias evaluation** — audit and improve face recognition models for fairness across ethnicities, gender, and age
* **Age-invariant face recognition** — match individuals across multi-year temporal gaps
